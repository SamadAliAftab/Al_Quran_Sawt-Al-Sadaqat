Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Al-Quran: Sawt Al-Sadaqat ("the App") is developed and maintained by Samad Ali Aftab. This page explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Summary

The App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share any data with third parties. Everything you read, listen to, or bookmark stays on your own device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account information — the App has no sign-up or login
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers — the App shows no ads
  • Analytics or usage-tracking data of any kind

Data Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded anywhere and is only accessible to the App itself:

  • Your bookmarks and reading position (which page/Surah/Ayah you last read or saved)
  • Your app preferences (theme, font, reciter, and similar settings)
  • The Quran text/audio/PDF content packs you choose to download

This data is removed automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually if you clear the App's storage from your device settings.

Internet Access

The App connects to the internet only to download optional Quran reading layouts, fonts, and audio recitation packs that you choose to download from within the App. These files are hosted on GitHub. No personal or device information is sent as part of these downloads beyond the standard, minimal technical information any internet connection requires (e.g. your device's IP address, which is standard for any network request and is not logged or used by us).

Permissions Used

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access — to download optional content packs
  • Network state — to check whether a connection is available before attempting a download

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out via the contact information listed on the App's Google Play Store page.
