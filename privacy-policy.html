Al-Quran: Sawt-Al-Sadaqat ("the App") is developed and maintained by Samad Ali Aftab. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how locally stored information is handled, and how optional online content downloads work.

Summary

The App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share any data with third parties. Everything you read, listen to, or bookmark stays on your own device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or account information — the App has no sign-up or login.
  • Your location.
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • Advertising identifiers — the App shows no ads.
  • Analytics or usage-tracking data of any kind.

Data Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only. This data is not uploaded by the App and is used only to provide the features you choose to use:

  • Your bookmarks and reading position (such as the last page, Surah, or Ayah you read or saved).
  • Your app preferences, including theme, font, reciter, and similar settings.
  • The Quran text, audio, and PDF content packs you choose to download.
This locally stored app data is removed when you uninstall the App, or when you clear the App's storage from your device settings.

Internet Access

The App connects to the internet only when you choose to download optional Quran reading layouts, fonts, audio recitation packs, or other supported content from within the App. These files are hosted on GitHub.

The App does not send personal information as part of these downloads. As with any internet connection, standard technical network information such as an IP address may be visible to the hosting service as part of normal network communication. We do not collect, use, or maintain such information for tracking purposes.

Permissions Used

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access — to download optional content packs.
  • Network state — to check whether a connection is available before attempting a download.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive permissions.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Samad Ali Aftab directly at samadali07@gmail.com, or through the contact information listed on the App's Google Play Store page.
